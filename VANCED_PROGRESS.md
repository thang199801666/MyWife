# YouTooBee enhanced playback progress

## Verified in this development pass

- Player ad handling now checks active ad state before clicking Skip, retries with a throttle, and counts once per ad episode.
- A synchronous Skip transition cannot seek the content video to its end.
- Ad acceleration restores mute, playback speed and repeat state, including when YouTube replaces the video element or blocking is disabled.
- Playback speed changes made during an ad survive restoration.
- Repeat and community segment seeking are suspended during player ads; ad time is excluded from content progress and queue-ended callbacks.
- DOM hiding avoids repeated identical style mutations.
- Shield injection runs at page commit and page completion, restricted to trusted YouTube pages.
- Trusted YouTube main-frame documents bypass ad-fragment matching so legitimate search terms/video IDs cannot blank the page; their advertising/tracking subresources remain filtered.
- An unloaded video placeholder cannot overwrite saved content progress; live streams with infinite duration send a finite bridge payload.
- Switching from fixed quality back to Auto now releases the previous player quality range.
- SponsorBlock prefix responses without a `hash` field are accepted by deriving SHA-256 from the returned video ID; wrong supplied hashes and unrelated candidate IDs remain rejected. Four JVM parser tests pass.
- Community segment skips become eligible again after a rewind or repeat pass, while normal playback does not count a skip twice.
- Page-ad filtering preserves the player's `.video-ads` / `.ytp-ad-module` controls container. Previously hiding that container made its Skip button fail the visibility check before clicking. A regression fixture covers idle-to-ad transition and successful Skip without seeking content.
- A manually replayed video can emit a new ended callback for queue advancement; duplicate callbacks while it stays ended remain suppressed.
- Player document injection now reapplies the active PiP layout and native play/pause intent across navigation, including queue transitions.
- Quality preferences are reapplied on reconnection, including when the metered profile changed during the preceding offline state.
- Configured native speed is reapplied to loaded/replaced content media and its defaultPlaybackRate; ad acceleration stays separate. Native speed commands update the script preference even before a media element is available.
- `node tests/adblock-script.cjs`: twenty player regression fixtures pass against the actual embedded script.
- `gradlew.bat testDebugUnitTest assembleDebug assembleRelease lintDebug --console=plain --max-workers=2`: successful; Lint has 0 errors and 185 existing warnings.
